# Running the order desk: a day-to-day guide

This is for the business owner and anyone who helps run the portal. Sign in at the site address
with your admin email. Everything below is in the **admin area** (the dark menu on the left; on a
phone or tablet it is the row of links at the top).

## Every morning: the dashboard
Open **Dashboard**. It shows:
- **Owed to you**: what restaurants still have to pay, and how many orders are overdue.
- **Owed to suppliers**: what you owe for orders already delivered, plus what will be owed when the
  rest arrive.
- **Chase today**: restaurants to call or remind today.
- **Waiting for approval**: new restaurants that registered.
- **Latest orders** and this month's sales and profit (before VAT).

A number on **Approvals** or **Payments & chasing** in the menu means something is waiting.

## A new restaurant registers
1. **Approvals** → open the restaurant.
2. Tick the **categories** they may see, and set their **default margin** if they get a special
   deal (leave it empty to use your standard margin from Settings).
3. **Approve account**. They can now sign in and see their prices. (Or **Reject** with a reason, which
   they will see.)

To add a restaurant yourself: **Customers → Add customer**, and tick "send a login invitation" if
they should order online.

## Setting prices
- **Products**: each pack size has a supplier, a **cost** (what the supplier charges you, before
  VAT) and a VAT rate. A size with no cost shows restaurants "Price on request" and cannot be ordered.
  **Products → Needs price** lists what is still missing.
- **Customers → a restaurant → Catalogue & prices**: per restaurant, choose categories, a margin
  per category, show or hide single products, or set a **fixed price** for a size. The preview shows
  the price they will see and why. **Copy from another restaurant** saves time.
- **Settings**: your standard margin, minimum order (below it a delivery charge is added),
  delivery charge, delivery days, and your business and **bank details** (shown on every order
  confirmation and invoice: fill these in before the first real order).

## When an order comes in
The order is split by supplier automatically, and each supplier sees its part in its own login
(no prices). Open **Orders** → the order:
- **Each supplier's part**: items, your cost, what you owe that supplier, and the delivery proof
  once delivered.
- **Change the order** (after speaking with the restaurant, before anything is delivered): change
  quantities, set 0 to take a line off, move a line to another supplier (enter that supplier's cost),
  or change the delivery charge. Saving tells the suppliers involved and the restaurant, and the
  invoice follows. After the first delivery the order is locked.
- **Cancel order**: give a reason (the restaurant sees it). Suppliers are told not to deliver and
  the invoice is marked void.
- **Invoice PDF**: the restaurant's invoice, ready to download or send.

## Deliveries
The supplier makes a **driver link** on its order and sends it to the driver (WhatsApp or text).
The driver opens it on a phone, takes a photo, adds the signed note or the customer's signature, and
submits. The order then shows as delivered, and the restaurant and you can see the proof.
You can also make a driver link or upload the proof yourself on the order page, and add a better
proof if a photo is poor.

## Money in: restaurants paying you
Restaurants pay by bank transfer with the reference **ORDER-number**. When money arrives:
1. Open the order (search the number in **Orders**).
2. **Record a payment**: amount, date, method, reference. Part payments are fine. A refund is
   recorded with the "refund" tick.
3. Paid in full clears the chase date.

## Chasing late payers
- On the order, set **Promised to pay by** and **Next chase**, and keep notes (only admins see them).
- **Payments & chasing → Chase today** lists everyone due a chase; **Overdue** lists those past
  the date they promised.
- **Send payment reminder** records a reminder and moves the next chase on three days.
  (Reminder emails go out once email sending is switched on; until then, call or message them.)

## Money out: paying suppliers
- **Suppliers → a supplier → To pay**: tick the delivered orders one bank transfer covered, enter
  the date and reference, and **Record payment and mark paid**.
- Or on an order: **Record a payment to [supplier]**, or **Mark paid**.
- **Payments & chasing → To pay suppliers** lists everything still owed.

When an order is delivered and both sides are paid, **Mark completed** on the order.

## Suppliers and logins
**Suppliers**: add a supplier, edit its details, invite a login for it (it then sees its orders
and makes driver links), or **Switch off** a supplier: restaurants can no longer order its
products, while orders already placed carry on. A login can be switched off on the same page.

## Who changed what
**Audit log** shows every change to prices, costs, payments, orders, customers and settings: who,
when, and the before and after values. Each order page also has its own timeline.

## Good to know
- Prices on an order never change after it is placed, even if you change costs or margins later.
- Profit = what the restaurant pays before VAT (goods + delivery) − your supplier costs.
- Emails (new orders to suppliers, confirmations, reminders) are recorded but **not sent yet**:
  email sending is switched on in a later step. Suppliers see new orders in their login meanwhile.
